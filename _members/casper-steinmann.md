Casper Steinmann leads the Steinmann Group at the [Department of Physics, Chemistry and Pharmacy (FKF)](https://fkf.sdu.dk), University of Southern Denmark, where he holds an Associate Professorship in Computational Drug Discovery and Formulation. He is also affiliated with the [Center for Pharmaceutical Data Science Education (CPDSE)](https://www.cpdse.dk/).

His research sits at the intersection of quantum chemistry, molecular simulation, and machine learning, with focus on synthesizability prediction, cyclodextrin inclusion complex chemistry, and ML for molecular design.

He holds a PhD from the University of Copenhagen and was previously Associate Professor of Computational Chemistry at Aalborg University for seven years.
