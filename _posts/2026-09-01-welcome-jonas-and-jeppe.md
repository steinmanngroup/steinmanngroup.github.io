We are happy to welcome two new PhD students to the Steinmann Group. [Jonas Jeppesen](/group/jonas-jeppesen/) and [Jeppe Falden](/group/jeppe-falden/) both started on September 1st, 2026, and will be with us until 2029.

Jonas works on our [Novo Nordisk Foundation Project Grant]({% post_url 2025-12-15-nnf-project-grant-light-chain %}) on the stability of immunoglobulin λ light-chain dimers. The proposal grew out of his master's thesis, so it is a pleasure to see him continue the work he helped start.

Jeppe is funded through the [Center for Pharmaceutical Data Science Education (CPDSE)](https://www.cpdse.dk/).

Welcome to both of you!
