The Steinmann Group is based at the [Department of Physics, Chemistry and Pharmacy (FKF)](https://fkf.sdu.dk), University of Southern Denmark, and affiliated with the [Center for Pharmaceutical Data Science Education (CPDSE)](https://www.cpdse.dk/).
We work at the intersection of quantum chemistry, molecular simulation, and machine learning with three areas of focus: <em>Synthesizability Prediction</em>, <em>Inclusion Complex Chemistry</em>, and <em>ML for Molecular Design</em>.
